CQ20: What is the step-by-step admission and graduation procedure for a graduate student (application, transcripts, fees, program of study, thesis/dissertation, commencement), and which university policy governs each step?

RESPONSE:
Here is an example of the step-by-step admission and graduation procedure for a graduate student at WSU:

**Step 1: Application**

* Submit a completed graduate admission application via the online portal.
* Include all required supporting credentials, such as transcripts and test scores (if applicable).
* The Graduate School will review the application and supporting documents to determine eligibility for admission.

**Step 2: Official Transcripts**

* Request that registrars of all previously attended colleges or universities send one official transcript directly to the Graduate School.
* The Graduate School will verify the authenticity of the transcripts and update student records accordingly.

**Step 3: Application Fee**

* Pay a $40 (domestic) or $70 (international) non-refundable application fee.
* McNair Scholars may have the fee waived with a Certification of Participation.

**Step 4: Program of Study**

* Complete a current and approved program of study on file in the Graduate School and/or a current degree audit in uAchieve.
* The student must also meet any additional program requirements, such as writing a statement of purpose or providing letters of recommendation.

**Step 5: Thesis/Dissertation (if required)**

* Submit test scores and/or documents such as letters of recommendation or statements of purpose if the program requires them.
* If a thesis or dissertation is required, the student must complete one copy of an approved thesis/dissertation for approval by the dean of the Graduate School and submission to OhioLINK.

**Step 6: Associate Director Determination**

* The Associate Director of Graduate Admissions reviews the recommendation and credentials and determines whether Graduate School admission requirements have been met.
* If the student meets all admission requirements, they are considered eligible for graduation.

**Step 7: Notification**

* The Graduate School notifies the applicant by letter of the admission decision.
* The notification will include any conditions or requirements that must be met before the student can register for classes.

**Step 8: Commencement**

* Register for commencement in a timely manner to ensure eligibility to participate.
* Graduates must also complete all degree requirements, including completing any required coursework, passing comprehensive exams (if applicable), and defending their thesis/dissertation (if applicable).
