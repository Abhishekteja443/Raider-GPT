CQ20: What is the step-by-step admission and graduation procedure for a graduate student (application, transcripts, fees, program of study, thesis/dissertation, commencement), and which university policy governs each step?

Here is the step-by-step admission and graduation procedure for a graduate student:

1. Submit application to the Graduate School via the online portal: University Policy 5010
2. Request official transcripts from all previously attended colleges or universities: University Policy 5020
3. Pay non-refundable application fee (domestic $40, international $70): University Policy 5070
4. Submit test scores and/or supporting materials as required by the program (if applicable): University Policy 5070
5. Have a current and approved program of study on file in the Graduate School: University Policy 5470
6. Complete at least 50 percent of credit hours at Wright State: University Policy 5380
7. Complete doctoral program requirements within ten years (Policy 5720.3): University Policy 5720
8. Complete a final comprehensive examination if required by the program: University Policy 5720
9. Demonstrate reading knowledge of a foreign language, as required by the program (if applicable): University Policy 5720
10. Complete graduate certificate requirements within five years (Policy 5810.2): University Policy 5810
11. Complete master's program requirements within seven calendar years: University Policy 5520
12. Achieve a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0 in all courses taken for graduate credit: University Policy 5370
13. Register for at least one graduate credit hour during the term in which a thesis/dissertation is defended: University Policy 5270
14. Present one copy of an approved thesis/dissertation for approval of the dean of the Graduate School and submission to OhioLINK (Policy 5515.1.9): University Policy 5515

For graduation, the following steps are also required:

15. Submit a completed Graduation Application online through WINGS Express: University Policy 5470
16. Pay $35.00 graduation application fee: University Policy 5470
17. Complete any additional requirements as specified by the program or university policy.

Note that some programs may have additional requirements, and applicants who wish to deviate from policies governing graduate students may submit a petition to the Graduate School requesting a waiver to a policy (University Policy 5080).
